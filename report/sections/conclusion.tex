In this report, we have considered a version of the 
capacitated facility location problem that considers
fluctuating demand over time, motivated by considering
the application of the Capacitated Facility Location Problem to energy systems modelling. 
We proposed a Lagrangian Relaxation Heuristic approach
to obtaining approximate solutions that decomposed the problem into subproblems, each solved through
a greedy algorithm. A classical subgradient algorithm  was also implemented, and iteratively 
the heuristic obtained upper and lower bounds 
which were iteratively tightened. It was found that this LR Heuristic achieves good approximations, 
although it takes prohibitively
longer than the standard solver to converge. This was established through applying this heuristic to 
a test case, applying it to solve an artificially constructed renewable energy systems site selection
problem that was constructed using data collected from UK government sources.
